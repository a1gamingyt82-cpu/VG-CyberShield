Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about bot-hosting.net? Here are the most common questions answered.

How do I keep my Discord bot online 24/7 without stopping? ▼
bot-hosting.net includes a built-in 24/7 Watchdog Supervisor daemon. Once you click "Start 24/7", the supervisor keeps the bot running in the background indefinitely. Even if Discord disconnects or an unhandled exception occurs, the supervisor automatically restarts the bot within 3 seconds.
How do I upload my bot files to the server? ▼
Go to the File Manager tab or drag and drop any file directly onto the browser window. You can upload individual files (.js, .py, .json, .env) or an entire .zip archive of your project — zip files are extracted automatically into your bot's folder!
Where do I set my Discord Bot Token? ▼
Click the "🔑 Set Discord Token" button in the top navigation or server header, or open the Environment (.env) tab. Paste your bot token from the Discord Developer Portal and click Save — your bot will immediately connect to Discord Gateway!
Why is there 0% buffering during voice playback? ▼
Our platform uses a low-latency FFmpeg pipeline with -analyzeduration 0, -probesize 32768, and background gapless pre-fetching. The audio packets start flowing to Discord voice in under 30 milliseconds with a 5000-frame anti-jitter buffer.
What programming languages and frameworks are supported? ▼
We natively support Node.js (v20, v22, v24) for discord.js v14, Eris, and custom bots, as well as Python 3 for discord.py, disnake, and pycord. You can run npm install directly in the web terminal!
